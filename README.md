## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **backend-focused software engineer** with **4 years of experience** building scalable, high-traffic web applications. My day-to-day work is in **Node.js, TypeScript, NestJS and MongoDB/PostgreSQL**, with solid **React / Next.js** skills that let me work comfortably across the full stack.

Right now I work on **multi-tenant, subscription-based SaaS platforms** and **medical / healthcare research systems**, where reliability, data integrity and security matter. I also build **AI/LLM-powered features** into production workflows, such as extracting structured data from documents and generating content automatically.

- 🏗️ I design **multi-tenant architectures** with tenant isolation, scalable data models and subscription workflows
- 🏥 I build backend systems for **medical research and healthcare** products
- 🤖 I integrate **GPT / LLM APIs** into production pipelines for document processing and content generation
- ⚙️ I build **background job systems, CRON automation and real-time features**
- 🔌 I integrate **payment, POS and messaging platforms** with proper fault handling and retries
- 🌍 Open to **international opportunities** and **part-time / freelance** work
